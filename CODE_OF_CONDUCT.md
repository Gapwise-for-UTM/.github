# Code of conduct

Gapwise welcomes constructive participation from students, developers, researchers and community contributors.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific and collaborative. In project spaces, participants should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give technical criticism with enough context to be useful;
- assume ordinary mistakes before malicious intent;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and sexualized or degrading conduct;
- keep security reports private when public disclosure could put users or systems at risk;
- respect maintainers' decisions about project scope, architecture and moderation even when disagreeing with them.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in Gapwise spaces:

- personal attacks, insults or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments based on personal characteristics;
- stalking, doxxing or unwanted disclosure of private information;
- deliberate disruption of issues, pull requests or project communication;
- knowingly posting secrets, credentials or private student data;
- using project channels to harass contributors or maintainers.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Keep it anchored to evidence, trade-offs and the needs of the product.

For campus-data disputes, distinguish verified sources from inference. For architecture disputes, identify the source-of-truth boundary being affected. For product behavior, provide a reproducible example whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or interactions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

If you need to report conduct privately, contact **support@gapwise.ca**. Security vulnerabilities should instead be sent to **security@gapwise.ca**.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in repositories and other official Gapwise project spaces operated by the maintainers.

Gapwise is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the University of Toronto.
